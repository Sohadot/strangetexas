# Strange Texas — Growth & Distribution Kit
## حقيبة النشر والتوزيع

> **لماذا تُرفض منشوراتك؟** ليست مشكلة جودة. معظم مجموعات فيسبوك تحجب المنشورات ذات الروابط الخارجية تلقائياً، وفيسبوك يخفض وصول أي منشور يُخرج الناس من المنصّة. الحلّ: انشري «أصلاً» (Native)، والرابط في **أول تعليق**.

## القاعدة الذهبية
1. **لا رابط في متن المنشور.** انشري القصة نفسها كمحتوى يعطي قيمة.
2. **الرابط في أول تعليق** (أو اكتبي: "link in comments 👇").
3. **اختمي بسؤال** يفتح نقاشاً (التفاعل يرفع الوصول).
4. انشري **صورة المقال** مع النصّ (المنشور المصوّر يصل أكثر).

## ترتيب القنوات (الأنسب لمحتواك)
1. **Pinterest** — الأقوى بلا منازع لهذا المحتوى البصري. لا حجب روابط، والبنّات تجلب زيارات لسنوات.
2. **صفحة فيسبوك خاصة** بـ Strange Texas — جمهور تملكينه، لا بوّابات مشرفين.
3. **Reddit**: r/Texas · r/TexasHistory · r/HighStrangeness — منشور نصّي سردي، الرابط في تعليق.
4. **SEO** — المحرّك طويل الأمد (بدأ فعلاً عبر خريطة الموقع).

### 🐾 The Lake Worth Monster
In the summer of 1969, six people drove to the Fort Worth police in the middle of the night to report the same thing: a seven-foot creature, covered in fur and scales, had jumped their car near Lake Worth. The police didn't laugh — they filed a report. The next night, a crowd watched it hurl a spare tire 500 feet down a hill. They called it the Goatman. It was never caught, never explained. What gets me: Fort Worth was paving over its last wild land that very summer. Ever heard this one? 👇
*Pinterest:* The 1969 Lake Worth Monster — Fort Worth's real "Goatman" police report. Texas cryptid folklore.

### 🌬️ The Blue Norther
Texans have a name for the strangest thing our sky does: the blue norther. It can be 75° in the afternoon — then you glance north and see a blue-black wall coming, and twenty minutes later it's below freezing. The old-timers called the worst ones "blue whistlers," and said: "there's nothing between here and the North Pole but a barbed-wire fence — and half of it's blown down." Anyone here ever been caught out in one? 👇
*Pinterest:* The Blue Norther — how a wall of cold shaped Texas. Weather folklore & the 1911 "day of two seasons."

### 🦉 La Lechuza
In South Texas, they say the barn owl was never only a bird. They call her La Lechuza — a giant owl with the face of an old woman. Some say she's a witch; some say if you wound the owl, an old woman turns up injured the next morning. But folklore never really asks whether she existed — it asks why a community needed her. Did you grow up hearing this one? 👇
*Pinterest:* La Lechuza — the owl-witch of South Texas & the Rio Grande Valley. Mexican-Texan folklore.

### 🐐 The Texas Chupacabra
Something has been killing goats in South Texas for decades — drained, no struggle, gone before sunrise. In 2007, a rancher near Cuero had a carcass DNA-tested. The result? A coyote with severe mange. Case closed… except the stories never stopped. Why does the Chupacabra refuse to die? 👇
*Pinterest:* The Texas Chupacabra — the legend, the DNA test, and why it won't die. South Texas cryptid.

### ✨ The Marfa Lights
For over 140 years, lights have appeared in the desert east of Marfa. They split, merge, dart sideways, and vanish. Science explains some of it with atmospheric refraction — but the first recorded sighting was in 1883, decades before car headlights existed. Nobody has fully explained them. Have you seen them? 👇
*Pinterest:* The Marfa Lights — West Texas's 140-year mystery. First recorded 1883.

### 🏚️ Five Texas Ghost Towns
Some Texas towns didn't fade — they stopped. Mercury mines emptied, railroads left, and the desert moved back in. You can still walk through Terlingua and Lobo today, and the old cemeteries still get candles. Which Texas ghost town have you visited? 👇
*Pinterest:* 5 Texas ghost towns worth the drive — Terlingua, Lobo & more. Abandoned Texas history.

### 🛸 Texas UFO Sightings
Long before Roswell, there was Aurora, Texas — 1897 — where townspeople said a "pilot not of this world" crashed and was buried in the local cemetery with Christian rites. The grave marker later vanished; nobody knows who took it. Texas has a UFO history unlike any other state. 👇
*Pinterest:* The 1897 Aurora, Texas UFO crash & other unexplained Texas sightings.

### 🔍 Unsolved Texas Murders
Along I-45 between Houston and Galveston, they've been finding bodies for over 50 years. They call it the Texas Killing Fields. Then there's the 1991 Austin yogurt shop murders — four girls, convictions overturned, still open. The families are still waiting. 👇
*Pinterest:* The Texas Killing Fields & the cases that were never solved. Texas true crime.

### 👻 The Grove, Jefferson
Built in 1861 in Jefferson, Texas, one Victorian home has unsettled its residents for over 160 years. One family fled in 1882 with only four words: "We can't live there." A "Lady in White" is still seen on the porch. Would you spend a night? 👇
*Pinterest:* The Grove in Jefferson — one of Texas's most haunted historic homes (built 1861).

*Pinterest:* The stunning caves beneath Texas — Sonora, Natural Bridge & Longhorn Cavern.
